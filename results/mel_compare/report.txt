Visual inspection of all 8 plots: predicted mels retain speech activity envelopes and low-frequency energy, but are heavily smoothed. Clean harmonics and changing formant bands are largely missing; predictions are neither constant nor random-looking speckle.
Scale: broadly compatible log-mel range; no clear evidence of a different log/normalization convention from statistics alone. Peaks are systematically weaker. Clamp changes at most 0.401% of bins and is unlikely to explain the failure.
Timing: predicted/clean frame counts differ by -56 to +36 frames (-0.650 to +0.418 seconds). Inference does not crop or pad mels to the clean length; only metric waveforms are truncated. Training uses clean target length for embedding interpolation.
All 8 post-clamp predicted mel hashes exactly match the earlier vocoder comparison.
Conclusion: excessive spectral smoothing and temporal mismatch are observed; a specific training/model root cause has not been established.
