W5 Layer Analysis Summary
=========================

All five HuBERT layers (6, 8, 9, 10, and 12) were trained and evaluated
on the Thai DSR task.

Comparison
----------

Layer | Val Loss | Best Epoch | Reconstructed STOI | Reconstructed PESQ | Reconstructed SNR (dB)
------|----------|------------|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------------
6     | 1.2936   | 18         | 0.2210             | 1.0899             | -0.5090
8     | 1.2852   | 22         | 0.2136             | 1.0979             | -0.6434
9     | 1.2885   | 16         | 0.2152             | 1.0973             | -0.5651
10    | 1.3115   | 24         | 0.1984             | 1.0930             | -0.6427
12    | 1.3094   | 22         | 0.2265             | 1.0864             | -0.6166

The validation-loss range is narrow (1.2852-1.3115), with an absolute
difference of 0.0262 between the best and worst results.

Conclusion
----------

Middle layers (6-12) perform similarly on Thai DSR.
Layer 8 has marginally best val loss but layer 9 is
retained as it has additional cosine similarity evidence.
